JONBA Video Backup

A personal desktop uploader for an orchestra score-video project. It stores checked video exports in Google Drive, verifies the saved files, and keeps current working files on the Mac.

Privacy policyUse and support

Privacy policy

Effective November 6, 2026

Google permissions

The uploader requests access only to Google Drive files and folders it creates, using the drive.file permission. It also requests basic sign-in and email information to confirm the correct Google account. It does not request permission to browse unrelated Drive files.

What the uploader uses

Generated videos, file names, file sizes, checksums and project folder identifiers are used only to upload, verify, retrieve and manage the user's project backups.

Where data goes

Video content is sent to the user's Google Drive. This uploader does not sell data, show advertising or send Drive content to AI services. The music project's separate research tools are not part of this uploader.

Storage and retention

Login credentials stay in a private, owner-readable directory on the user's Mac. Current projects, source recordings, scores and code remain local. Older generated-video copies may be removed from the Mac only after the cloud copy has been verified. When the same generated export is replaced, the uploader keeps the two latest verified versions. It can move older unused versions to Drive Trash after checking the retained copies. It never empties Drive Trash or removes original scores or recordings.

Your control

You can stop the background worker, revoke the app's connection from your Google Account and delete backups from your own Drive. Restoring an archived video downloads and caches it locally for further use.

Limited Use

The uploader follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Use and support

This is a personal tool, not a public video service. It is provided without a guarantee of cloud availability or musical alignment. A successful storage check does not establish permission to redistribute a recording.

For support, contact the app owner through the support email displayed on Google's consent screen.
